## Direct observations

- `1000000391.jpg` makes the installed PCB's `-0604` part-number suffix legible.
  The preceding characters are obscured by installed connectors, so this
  strongly corroborates the owner-reported `9067-0604` rather than constituting
  an unobstructed photographic read of the complete part number.
- `1000000392.jpg` and `1000000395.jpg` visibly carry `Copyright (C) 2002, 2003
  Bixby Energy Systems` on the installed board.
- `1000000394.jpg` resolves the main-board J3 housing and all four contacts. Its
  black, single-row, four-contact form and edge location agree with the vendor
  release notes and the related `9067-0404` diagram.
- `1000000394.jpg` also makes the nearby `LM1815N` package marking readable.
  Its electrical relationship to J3 or any sensor path has **not** been traced;
  proximity alone is not a net-level conclusion.
- The images document the installed component side and harness positions, but
  not the solder side, connector continuity, trace routing under components, or
  oscillator marking.

The auxiliary output PCB also has its own `J3` designator. References to the
computer/service port in OpenMaxFire mean the black four-contact **main-board
J3** shown in `1000000394.jpg`, not the auxiliary-board connector visible in
`1000000398.jpg`.

## Limits and safety

These photographs establish physical identity and placement only. They do not
identify J3 ground, TX, RX, supply, signaling voltage, polarity, isolation, or
pin order. Wire color is not a substitute for continuity testing. The stove
contains exposed 120 V AC circuits; power must be disconnected before access,
and the side panel must be installed for operation as required by the appliance
warning label.
